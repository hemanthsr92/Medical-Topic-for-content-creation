## Series 8: Quick Quizzes (comment drivers, easy to make)

- **"S or O?" rapid-fire:** a follow-up to the SOAP reel.
- **"Spot the error" prescriptions:** a follow-up to the iron reel.
- **"Name the sign" from a photo:** use consented images or illustrations only.
- **"ECG in 5 seconds":** a strip with three answer choices.
- **"Which drug interaction?":** two drugs and a question.

## Which to make next

1. **IV fluid orders.** Juniors write them on every shift and often get them wrong. The lesson is easy to save, and the only clinical fact to check is the maintenance fluid formula.
2. **Calling your senior with ISBAR.** It's relatable and shareable, has few accuracy risks, and links directly to the SOAP series.
3. **ABG in 60 s.** It's always popular for saves. Put a worked example in the carousel.
4. **Paracetamol dosing.** It's high-impact for safety and easy to fact-check against BNF/BNFc.
5. **Things nobody tells you before internship.** Use it to reach new followers between the teaching reels.

## Content mix

- **Teaching reels (about 60%).** These earn saves and authority.
- **Quizzes (about 20%).** These earn comments.
- **Relatable intern-life reels (about 20%).** These earn shares and new followers.

Post 3 a week and keep each series to 4–6 parts.

**Accuracy:** Any dose, threshold or procedure topic needs the same treatment as the iron reel: list the sources, flag where guidelines differ, and get faculty review before posting. That applies especially to fluids, insulin, potassium, paediatric dosing and procedures.
